Lianrui Geng

Computer Science, UNC Chapel Hill

I study how AI systems decide whom to ask, what to ask, and when to stop.

Most of my work concerns decisions made under scarcity: feedback that is expensive to collect, and compute that runs out. I am a master's student at UNC Chapel Hill, working with Zhun Deng. Before that, I studied computer science and mathematics at the University of Utah.
